Our Story

A quiet perch above Buea

La-Maliva Vista began with a simple idea: a guest house that feels like a home in the hills — warm, unhurried, and honest about what it offers.

Where we are

The mountain on one side, the town on the other.

We sit on the cool slopes of Mount Cameroon, a short drive from Buea town and the road up to the mountain. Mornings here are mist and birdsong; afternoons open into a wide view over the rooftops.

Fifteen rooms, a small kitchen that cooks what the market gives us, and a team drawn from the neighbourhood. Nothing is outsourced to a distant brand — the care is ours.

What we hold to

Small hotel, clear promises.

Breakfast is included, the price you are quoted is the price you pay, and the people who welcome you are the people who live here.

Slow mornings

Breakfast is served when you wake, not when a timetable says so. Fresh fruit, eggs, bread and coffee from the highlands.

Real hospitality

Our team is local. Ask about the trails, the market, or the best spot for a sunset and you will get a straight answer.

Honest books

Every payment is recorded the moment it is taken. Your bill at check-out matches what you were told at check-in.
