Welcome to Paradise

A sanctuary on the slopes of Mount Fako

Tucked into the quiet heart of GRA Bokwaongo, La-Maliva Vista is a boutique hideaway where thoughtful design meets the famously warm hospitality of Cameroon. Wake to mist over the mountain, linger over breakfast in the garden, and let our concierge craft your perfect day — from the trails of Mount Cameroon to the markets of Buea.

Every one of our fifteen rooms and suites is individually styled, refreshed daily, and attended around the clock by a team that remembers your name.
