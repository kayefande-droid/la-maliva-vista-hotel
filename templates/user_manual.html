Guest User Manual

Everything you need to use the La-Maliva Vista app and website — nothing else to learn.

Getting the app

  • Android: open the Downloads page and tap Download for Android, then open the APK to install.
  • Windows: download the ZIP, unzip it and run lamaliva_app.exe.
  • Phone or PC without installing? Use the website — you can also install it as an app from your browser (“Add to Home Screen” / “Install”).

Your account

  • Create your account on the website’s sign-up page — the same username and password work in the app.
  • Sign in on any device; your bookings and receipts follow you everywhere.
  • Change your password anytime from the app’s Account tab or the website menu.
  • Forgot your password? Call reception on (+237) 679-915-967 and we’ll reset it.

Booking a room

  • Open Rooms (app or website) to see live photos, prices in FCFA and availability.
  • Pick your dates, choose the number of nights and confirm — you’ll get an instant confirmation with a reference number.
  • Your booking appears under Bookings in the app and in “My Bookings” on the website.

Receipts & invoices

  • Every booking has three buttons: Receipt (quick view), Download (saves a branded PDF), and Print.
  • On the website, open any booking and use Print Invoice or Download PDF.
  • Invoices include the hotel’s address and reception number — perfect for expense claims.

Using the app offline

  • Room photos, prices and the menu are saved on your device — they open even with no internet.
  • An amber banner appears when you’re offline; it disappears automatically the moment the connection returns and everything refreshes itself.
  • Booking and receipt actions need internet; everything else works anytime.

Make it yours

  • Six design styles: Royal Navy, Sunset Amber, Emerald Royale, Plum Noir, Ocean Teal, Graphite Noir — app drawer → Change app theme, or the color dots in the website sidebar.
  • Pick Light, Dark, or follow your system’s appearance.
  • Each style has its own animated background — waves, embers, petals, orbs, bubbles or streaks.

Notifications

  • The bell in the app shows messages from the La-Maliva team and the hotel — plus your booking and update alerts.
  • A small number badge means unread news; opening the bell marks it read.
  • Keep notifications enabled so booking confirmations reach you instantly.

Keeping the app up to date

  • The app checks for updates on its own and quietly notifies you when a new version ships.
  • You can also check manually: Account → Check for updates.
  • If an update exists, tap Download — it takes you straight to the official download; install it and you’re current.

Find & contact us
